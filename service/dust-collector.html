Lesca Tech designs and installs high-efficiency industrial dust collection systems tailored to the unique requirements of the woodworking, pharmaceutical, chemical, and metal processing industries. Our systems are engineered to capture particulate matter at the source, ensuring a clean working environment and strict compliance with environmental air quality regulations.

Dust filtration technology

We combine high-performance filtration media with robust structural design to capture sub-micron dust particles effectively.

Cyclonic Pre-Separation

For high-volume dust loads, we integrate heavy-duty cyclone separators that use centrifugal force to remove larger particles before they reach the main filters, significantly extending filter life.

Pulse-Jet Cleaning Systems

Our baghouses feature automated pulse-jet cleaning mechanisms that use timed bursts of high-pressure compressed air to dislodge dust from filter surfaces, maintaining constant airflow without system shutdown.

"The pulse-jet dust collector installed by Lesca Tech has transformed our workshop's air quality. The automatic cleaning system works flawlessly, and we haven't seen any visible dust emission since it went live."

Why choose our dust collectors

We provide modular and scalable solutions that prioritize energy efficiency and operator safety.

Optimized Extraction Power

We design custom suction hoods and ducting layouts to ensure maximum capture velocity at the point of dust generation while minimizing static pressure loss.

  • Explosion-proof safety features (ATEX)
  • Durable polyester and PTFE filter media

Eco-Friendly Air discharge

Our systems achieve 99.9% filtration efficiency for particles as small as 0.3 microns, allowing for clean air discharge back into the facility or the atmosphere.

  • Reduced energy consumption
