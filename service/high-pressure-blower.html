Lesca Tech specializes in the design and manufacture of high-performance industrial blowers engineered for high-pressure air handling applications. Our blowers are built to deliver consistent airflow and high static pressure, making them ideal for pneumatic conveying, furnace air supply, and industrial ventilation where resistance is high.

Our blower manufacturing process

Every high-pressure blower we produce undergoes rigorous testing to ensure it meets demanding industrial performance standards.

Precision Dynamic Balancing

All impellers are dynamically balanced on digital balancing machines. This ensures vibration-free operation, reduces bearing wear, and extends the overall lifespan of the motor and drive system.

Aerofoil Impeller Design

Our engineers utilize advanced aerofoil blade profiles that maximize static efficiency. Our high-pressure designs are optimized to deliver maximum head with minimum power consumption.
