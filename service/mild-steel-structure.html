At Lesca Tech, we provide comprehensive heavy fabrication services for industrial infrastructure, plant sheds, and support systems. Our Mild Steel (MS) structural solutions are engineered for maximum durability, load-bearing capacity, and compliance with Indian Standard (IS) codes, ensuring safe and reliable operations for heavy industries.

Our heavy fabrication process

We follow a systematic workflow from technical detailing to precision welding and on-site erection.

Engineering & Detailing

Our team develops detailed fabrication drawings based on IS 800 standards, ensuring every section, joint, and bolt is optimized for structural integrity and material efficiency.

Precision Fabrication & Welding

Fabrication is carried out by certified welders using modern MIG/TIG welding machines. We perform rigorous quality checks including DP tests and dimensional inspections before finishing.

Why choose our structural solutions

We combine heavy engineering expertise with advanced surface treatment technologies to provide structures that last for decades in demanding environments.

High Load-Bearing Capacity

Our structures are designed for heavy industrial loads, including overhead crane supports, mezzanine floors, and heavy equipment foundations.

  • Multi-Layer Epoxy Painting
