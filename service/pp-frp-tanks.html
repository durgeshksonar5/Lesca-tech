At Lesca Tech, we specialize in the design and fabrication of premium PP FRP Tanks. These dual-laminate vessels combine the exceptional chemical resistance of Polypropylene (PP) with the high structural strength of Fiber Reinforced Plastic (FRP), creating a superior storage solution for the most aggressive industrial chemicals.

Our dual-laminate fabrication process

Every PP FRP tank we manufacture undergoes a rigorous dual-material bonding process to ensure industrial-grade durability and safety.

Thermoplastic Lining

The inner shell is fabricated using high-grade PPH (Polypropylene Homopolymer) sheets, meticulously welded to provide a seamless, corrosion-proof barrier.

Structural Reinforcement

The outer shell is reinforced with multiple layers of high-strength glass fiber and resin (FRP) using advanced winding techniques for maximum pressure resistance.

PP FRP Tank FAQs.

A dual-laminate tank uses a thermoplastic liner (like PP) as the primary chemical barrier, which is then structurally reinforced with a secondary layer of FRP (Fiberglass). This provides the "best of both worlds" for corrosion resistance and strength.

PP FRP tanks are widely used for the storage and processing of highly corrosive chemicals, including acids like H2SO4, HCl, and various plating solutions in the chemical, pharma, and metal finisher industries.

Standard PP FRP tanks safely handle temperatures up to 80°C - 90°C. For higher temperature requirements, we offer specialized resins and grades to ensure operational safety.

Yes, our tanks are manufactured using UV-stabilized resins in the outer FRP layer, making them perfectly suitable for long-term outdoor installation in harsh environments.
