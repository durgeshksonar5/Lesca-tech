Lesca Tech specializes in the design and manufacture of Industrial FRP (Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic) Piping Systems, providing a superior alternative to traditional metallic and concrete pipes. Our piping is engineered to handle the transport of highly corrosive chemicals, abrasive slurries, and industrial effluents with zero risk of structural degradation or internal scaling.

Advanced piping fabrication

We focus on hydraulic efficiency and mechanical durability to ensure a maintenance-free fluid transport system.

High-Speed Filament Winding

Our pipes are produced using state-of-the-art filament winding machines. This process creates a high-density composite wall with exceptional axial and hoop strength, allowing our pipes to withstand significant surge pressures and vacuum loads.

Resin-Rich Internal Liner

Every pipe features a mirror-smooth, resin-rich internal liner (0.5mm to 2.5mm thick). This liner is specifically formulated to provide maximum chemical resistance while minimizing friction loss, which reduces pumping energy requirements.

Advantages of our FRP pipes

Our composite piping offers a unique combination of structural strength and complete chemical inertness.

Exceptional Hydraulic Flow

The Hazen-Williams coefficient for our FRP pipes is approximately 150. This hydraulic smoothness remains constant over the pipe’s life, preventing energy loss due to buildup or pitting.

  • Lower pumping costs
  • No internal scaling or fouling
  • Efficient long-distance transport

Simplified Installation

FRP pipes are 75% lighter than steel and 90% lighter than concrete. This drastically reduces the need for heavy lifting equipment and speeds up site installation.

  • Ease of field jointing
  • Lower structural support costs
  • UV-Protected external surface
