Centrifugal Blower FAQs.

Our industrial blowers are designed to handle static pressures ranging from 50mm to 500mm WG. For extremely high-pressure needs, we offer specialized multi-stage centrifugal blowers.

FRP blowers should be chosen whenever the air contains corrosive gases, acids, or high moisture levels that would cause a steel blower to rust or corrode. MS blowers are better suited for non-corrosive, high-temperature, or clean air applications.

Regular maintenance involves checking belt tension, lubricating bearings, and ensuring the impeller is clean. We provide detailed maintenance manuals and optional annual maintenance contracts (AMC).

Backward-curved impellers are best for high efficiency and clean air. Radial blades are ideal for air containing dust or particulate matter as they have self-cleaning properties that prevent buildup.
